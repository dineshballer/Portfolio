Section head — 4 ideas to balance the right side

Same left-side content (Problem Area). Different right-side treatments. Pick what feels right.

Idea 1 · Big Section Number

Editorial section number

Big numeric counter (01 / 02 / 03) acts as a visual landmark. Looks like a magazine spread or design pub. Most polished, simplest to add. No additional content required.

Problem area

Lost in the back-and-forth

Mortgage companies and subserviced clients lack a way to manage their loan inquiries after the initial request is sent. Because there is no central place to track progress, exchange documents, or message teams, clients are forced to dig through hundreds of emails to find specific case details or files.

Idea 2 · Pull Quote

Research quote in the margin

Pull a key user-research line and put it next to the section it belongs to. Adds voice + qualitative weight. Lets you reuse the Ivan/Glenda quotes contextually rather than dumping them all in one block.

Idea 3 · Stat Card

Key figure / context stat

A bold supporting metric that makes the problem tangible. Eye-catching, gives the recruiter a "tweet-able" data point at every section. Works especially well next to Problem and Validation sections.

Idea 4 · Icon Tag List

Mini icon-tag preview

Three small icon tags previewing the problems below (or the solution sub-features). Acts as a "table of contents" for the section, so the reader knows what's coming. Functional + visual.
