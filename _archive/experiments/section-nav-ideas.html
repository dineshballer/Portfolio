Sticky section nav — 3 ideas

Three takes on a left-side anchor menu for the case studies. Each is shown alongside a simulated case-study panel so you can compare. Pick one and I'll wire it into both case studies (HTML + Figma).

Idea A · Anchor list

Minimal text list with left accent bar

Closest to Linear, Stripe docs, and Vercel. Just section names, muted by default. The active section pulls a thin green accent bar on the left and the label gets darker + bolder. Cleanest, most familiar pattern. No icons, no numbers, no decoration.

Solution

A central hub for real-time oversight

I built a central hub that brings every loan inquiry into one clear, organized view. Clients no longer have to dig through their inboxes to find out if a case is moving forward.

Everything is right there, updated in real time so nobody is left guessing about the status of their work.

Idea B · Numbered

Numbered steps with filled active dot

Treats the case study like a guided walkthrough — each section has a numeric chip. The active step's chip fills with the brand green; everything else stays understated. Slightly more "designed" feel, signals progression. Works well if the recruiter wants a sense of "where am I in the story."

Idea C · Progress line

Vertical progress line with dot per section

A continuous vertical track on the left. Each section is a dot on the line; sections you've already scrolled past fill in green. The active section's dot has a soft green halo. Adds a subtle sense of progress through the page — feels like reading a long article. Slightly more visual than A, less labeled than B.
