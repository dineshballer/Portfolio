Part 1 · Annotated screen

Validation at the door, explained in place

The validation summary sits on top. The numbered markers explain each feature when you hover, tap, or tab to them. Nothing leaves the page, and it works without a mouse.

Validation ✓13 / 13 columns detected ✓147 / 147 PAs matched by loan number ✓Saved mapping recognized No blocking errors
Validation at the door The moment the file lands, the portal checks the column structure against the expected template. 13 of 13 columns detected, format OK. A wrong report version or missing field is caught here, not three days later.
Purchase Advices auto-matched Every PA is matched to a loan number on upload. 147 of 147 matched means nothing is orphaned downstream in reconciliation.
Saved mapping recognized The portal recognizes the client's saved column mapping, the Standard 15-day template, so repeat submitters skip remapping. One click to confirm, or switch.
Loan numbers parsed automatically 147 loan numbers are read straight from the spreadsheet, so the analyst never rekeys them. The count cross-checks against the PA batch above.

Try it: hover, tap, or press Tab then Enter on a marker. On the live page I'll fine-tune marker positions and write one tooltip per real feature.
